# Watch room schematics

- [Floor plan](floorplan.svg) shows the round watch room from above. Put the CHUD terminal forward against the curved desk, with the ocean behind it. The left window opens directly into the room, the stairwell on the right leads down to the ground door, and the exterior balcony door is directly behind the player.
- [Camera plan](cameras.svg) uses a single seated pivot. `cam_desk` faces forward, `cam_window` turns left, `cam_stairs` turns right and down, and `cam_balcony` turns 180° to face the rear door and railing. Magenta markers locate Ram's partial reveals.
- [Tower sections](section.svg) show the left window opposite the right stairwell and the ocean-facing desk opposite the balcony. The base cottage remains an attached annex. The lantern room sits above the watch room.

## Scale

One drawing unit equals one meter. The watch room is 6.0 m across inside and 3.0 m tall, the seated eye height is 1.2 m, the balcony projects 1.2 m, and Ram is about 1.9 m tall. The watch room at +6.0 m and the balcony and door sizes are blocking targets for review. The left-to-right cut is offset through the window and stairs. The front-to-rear cut runs through the terminal, chair, and balcony door.

The front ocean glazing is fixed. Ram's three entry points are the left window, ground door below the right stairs, and rear balcony door.
