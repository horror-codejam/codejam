# Watch room schematics

- [Floor plan](floorplan.svg) shows the round watch room from above. Put the CHUD terminal forward against the curved desk, with the ocean behind it. The chair sits close to the keyboard. The left window opens directly into the room, the right floor hatch covers a fixed ladder, and the exterior balcony door is directly behind the player.
- [Camera plan](cameras.svg) uses a single seated pivot. `cam_desk` faces forward, `cam_window` turns left, `cam_hatch` turns right and down, and `cam_balcony` turns 180° to face the rear door and railing. Magenta markers locate Ram's partial reveals.

## Scale

One drawing unit equals one meter. The watch room is 6.0 m across inside and 3.0 m tall, the seated eye height is 1.2 m, the balcony projects 1.2 m, and Ram is about 1.9 m tall. The hatch opening is a provisional 1.00 × 0.96 m. Dashed lines show the ladder below the closed lid. The hinge is on the wall side. The player can press down on the lid from the watch-room floor.

The front ocean glazing is fixed. Ram's three entry points are the left window, the right floor hatch, and rear balcony door.
