# The Keeper ending stills

The two shots were assembled in fresh Blender scenes. The cage hall, cages, posed captives, rescue boat stern, and lighthouse were modeled for the ending. The boat camera is at the player's eye level on the deck.

The following assets are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) under its [CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license). Both Blender files pack their textures.
